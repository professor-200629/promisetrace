# Seller interview script (5 independent sellers paid by overseas clients)

**Status: not yet conducted. Findings section is intentionally empty.**

Who: freelancers or one-person studios (design, development, writing, video) who received at least one payment from an overseas client in the last 6 months, ideally through PayPal. Recruit from different services; avoid friends who already know the idea.

Format: 20–25 minutes, video or voice, notes taken with permission. Do not show PromiseTrace until Part C.

## Part A — the last misunderstanding (10 min)

1. Tell me about the last time a client and you disagreed about what was included or delivered. What was the job, and what was the price?
2. Where had the scope been agreed? (chat, email, call, contract, platform)
3. What exactly was disputed — a file format, a count, a deadline, revisions, quality, something else?
4. How did it end? Did any money get refunded, held or disputed through PayPal or a card?
5. How much time did it take you, roughly? What did it cost you (money, rating, the client relationship)?

## Part B — how you collect evidence today (7 min)

6. If PayPal or the client asked you to prove what was agreed and delivered, what would you send?
7. Where do those pieces live today? How long would it take to put them together?
8. Have you ever opened or answered a PayPal dispute? What did PayPal ask for?
9. Do you write a brief or contract before starting? Who writes it, and does the client confirm it?

## Part C — reaction to the shared brief and delivery link (6 min)

Show: one screen of the approved brief with source links, and one screen of the requirement table after delivery (missing SVG).

10. Would your clients accept being sent a link to approve a brief before paying? What would stop them?
11. Which part, if any, would you actually use: the approved brief, the payment link, the delivery checks, the exported record?
12. What would you need to trust it? What would make you stop using it?
13. If this cost money, who should pay — you, the client, or neither — and what would feel reasonable? (Note the answer; do not suggest a price.)

## Closing (2 min)

14. Who else should I talk to?
15. May I contact you if we run a small pilot?
